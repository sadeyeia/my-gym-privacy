Privacy Policy — My Gym

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the My Gym mobile application ("the App") handles your information. We built My Gym with a simple principle: your data stays on your device.

1. Data Collection

My Gym does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data on external servers. The App runs entirely on your device without requiring an account, login, or internet connection.

2. What We Store Locally

The App stores the following information only on your device using standard local storage:

  • Workout routines you create
  • Workout history and set logs
  • Favorite exercises
  • Body measurements you enter
  • Progress photos you add
  • App preferences (language, rest timer, training days)

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with any third party. If you uninstall the App, all this data is permanently deleted.

3. Permissions

My Gym requests no sensitive runtime permissions. It does not access your:

  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Camera or microphone
  • Files or media storage (photos are accessed only when you actively pick them)
  • Phone or SMS

4. Third-Party Services

The App contains no:

  • Advertisements
  • Analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media integrations
  • Any external data collection services

The only external interaction is the "Watch form tutorial" button, which opens a YouTube search in your browser or the YouTube app. When you use this feature, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that interaction. My Gym does not receive or process any data from this.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages. Since we collect no data from anyone, we also collect no data from children under 13. The App is not designed to be used by children without adult supervision for physical activities.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device software up to date.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible directly inside the App.
  • Export: Use the "Export data" option in Settings to save a JSON copy.
  • Delete: Use the "Clear" buttons in the App, or uninstall the App to remove all data permanently.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Medical Disclaimer

My Gym provides general fitness and exercise information only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition.
